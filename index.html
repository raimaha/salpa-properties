About us

A family-run developer with long-term values.

Salpa Properties Limited was founded in Folkestone in 2026 to buy land and develop high-quality new-build homes across Kent and the South East. We are a family-run company, and we bring the same care to every project that we would want in our own home.

We work closely with landowners, architects, contractors and local authorities to turn the right land into homes people are proud to live in – planned responsibly, built to a high standard and delivered with clear communication at every stage.

Leadership

The people behind Salpa.

KR

Kalpana Rai

Founder & Director

Kalpana founded Salpa Properties and leads the company's strategy, land acquisition and project delivery. She brings a strong design background to every scheme: she holds a BArch in Architecture from KEC, Nepal (2010) and a Master's in Urban Design from the University of Sheffield (2014), and has years of experience in UK architectural design, technical drawings and 3D modelling. Her particular interest in housing design is at the heart of Salpa's focus on well-designed new homes.

SR

Suman Chandra Rai

Director – Projects & Investment

Suman leads Salpa's projects and investment, from securing finance to overseeing delivery on site. He retired as a Captain after 27 years of distinguished service in the British Army's Brigade of Gurkhas, where he led teams, planned complex operations and was trusted with significant responsibility. He brings that discipline, integrity and leadership to every Salpa development and to every partnership with investors.

MR

Maha Rai

Company Secretary

Maha oversees governance, financial controls and company administration at Salpa. He holds a PhD in Physics and brings an analytical, evidence-based approach to every decision. He has extensive senior leadership experience in strengthening governance and internal financial controls, financial planning and budget oversight, securing and managing multi-year funding, negotiating leases, and building partnerships with local authorities and public bodies. He brings that rigour and accountability to every Salpa project.
